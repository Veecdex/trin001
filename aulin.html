A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions? We’ve got you.

A quick look at how Aulin helps independent stores get online and run their digital operations.

Is Aulin free to use?

You can get started without a fixed monthly subscription. Aulin makes money when you make a sale online, so we grow alongside your business. Payment processing or other applicable transaction fees may still apply.

How do I receive money when a customer pays?

Customer payments are processed through the payment setup connected to your Aulin store. Payouts are sent to the Nigerian bank account linked to your business, typically on the next business day, subject to payment processing and settlement schedules.

What is Aulin?

Aulin brings together the digital tools stores need to present their products online, manage orders and offer customers a smoother shopping experience.

Who can use Aulin?

Aulin is built for supermarkets, grocery stores and other independent retailers that want to sell online while keeping their own business identity.

Do I need technical skills to set up my store?

Aulin is designed to make setup straightforward, with guided tools for your business profile, product listings and online storefront—without needing to build a website from scratch.

Can customers browse products and place orders online?

Yes. Aulin gives customers a place to discover products, place orders and receive order updates, while your team manages orders through your business tools.

Does Aulin provide delivery services?

Aulin helps your team coordinate delivery workflows and track orders. You can work with your own delivery staff or available delivery partners, depending on your setup.

How do I get started?

Start by sharing your business details through the onboarding process. You can then set up your profile, add products and prepare your online store.
